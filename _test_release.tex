This report presents the work carried out during a final year project for obtaining the Engineer degree in Networks and Information Systems at the Faculty of Sciences and Technology of Marrakech. The internship was conducted at TAMTAM International, a Belgian company specializing in digital solutions for financial professionals, with the primary objective of developing and enhancing a comprehensive web and mobile event management application specifically designed for Belgian fiduciaries.

The platform provides centralized, intuitive management of professional events while ensuring scalability and user-friendly interfaces.
